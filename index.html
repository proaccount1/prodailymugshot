About the app

Pro DailyMugShot helps you keep track of every cup you drink: espresso, latte, cappuccino, tea, matcha and more. Log the drink type, size and temperature, optionally mark the place on a map, and see your daily caffeine intake against your own limit. Weekly and monthly statistics and a heatmap show your coffee habits over time.

Frequently asked questions

How do I log a cup?

On the Home tab, tap the add button, choose the drink, size and temperature, and save. You can also pick the date and time and add the place where you had it.

Why does the app ask for my location?

Location is optional. It is only used while you are adding a cup, to show your position on the map and suggest nearby cafés. If you decline, you can still log cups and search for a place manually. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

How do I back up my data or move it to a new iPhone?

Open the Settings tab and tap Save data to iCloud. On your new device, sign in with the same Apple Account and tap Load data from iCloud. Make sure iCloud is turned on for the device.

How do I change my profile photo?

Open the Settings tab and tap the avatar. The app only gets access to the photo you choose.

How is the daily caffeine amount calculated?

Each drink has a typical caffeine amount that is adjusted for the cup size. These are approximate values. Real caffeine content depends on the beans, the café and how the drink is prepared. You can set your own daily limit in the Settings tab.

How do I delete my data?

All your data is stored on your device. Deleting the app removes it. If you saved a backup to iCloud, see the Privacy Policy for how to remove it.

Pro DailyMugShot is not a medical app. The caffeine figures are estimates for general information only and are not medical advice. If you have health concerns related to caffeine, please consult a doctor.
