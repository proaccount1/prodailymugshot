Privacy Policy

Pro DailyMugShot · Effective November 6, 2026

Support Privacy Policy

In short: Pro DailyMugShot does not collect, store or share any of your personal data on our servers. We do not have servers. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your device, and in your personal iCloud only if you choose to back it up.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Pro DailyMugShot iOS app ("the app", "we", "us"), developed by Ahmet Yoner, handles your information.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not require registration, does not contain analytics or crash reporting tools, does not show ads, and does not include any third-party SDKs that collect data.

2. Data stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following information locally on your iPhone:

  • the cups you log: date and time, drink type, size and temperature;
  • an optional place for each cup: its name and map coordinates, if you choose to add one;
  • your settings, such as the daily caffeine limit;
  • an optional profile photo that you choose.

This data never leaves your device unless you use the iCloud backup described below. We have no access to it.

3. Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location only while it is open, when you are adding a cup or choosing a place. It is used to show your position on the map and to find nearby cafés. Your location is not tracked in the background and is not sent to us.

Maps, place search and address lookup are provided by Apple Maps (MapKit). When you use these features, the relevant requests are processed by Apple in accordance with Apple's Privacy Policy. Location access is optional, and you can change it at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

4. Photos

When you set a profile photo, the app uses the system photo picker. The app receives only the single photo you select and does not have access to the rest of your photo library. The photo is stored only on your device.

5. iCloud backup

The app lets you manually save a backup of your cup history to your own iCloud account and restore it later. This data is stored in your private iCloud storage, operated by Apple, and is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. We cannot see or access it. The backup happens only when you tap "Save data to iCloud".

6. Deleting your data

Because all data is stored on your device, you are always in control of it:

  • deleting the app from your iPhone removes all locally stored data;
  • data saved to iCloud is linked to your Apple Account. You can remove it by turning off iCloud for the app or by managing your iCloud data in your device settings. You can also contact us and we will explain how to do it.

7. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, because we do not collect any.

8. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.

10. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at ahmetyoner6@gmail.com.
